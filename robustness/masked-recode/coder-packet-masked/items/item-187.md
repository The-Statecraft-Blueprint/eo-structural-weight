# item-187

By virtue of and pursuant to the authority vested in me by section 179 of the Revised Statutes (5 U.S.C., sec. 6), I hereby authorize and direct Dr. W. C. Mendenhall, Director of the Geological Survey, to perform the duties of the Secretary of the Interior during the absence or sickness from [DATE] to [DATE], inclusive, of the Secretary of the Interior, the Under Secretary of the Interior, the First Assistant Secretary of the Interior and the Assistant Secretary of the Interior.
Executive Order No. [EO-A] of [DATE], is modified accordingly.

# item-200

By virtue of the authority vested in me by sections 55 (a) and 603 of the Internal Revenue Code ([STAT], 111), it is hereby ordered that corporation statistical transcript cards prepared by the Bureau of Internal Revenue from corporation income and declared value excess-profits tax returns made under the Internal Revenue Code, as amended, for any taxable year ending after [DATE], and before [DATE], shall be open to inspection by the Office of Price Administration; such inspection to be in accordance and upon compliance with the rules and regulations prescribed by the Secretary of the Treasury in Treasury Decision 5173, approved [DATE], which relates to the inspection of similar cards for taxable years ending after [DATE], and before [DATE].
This order shall be published in the
Federal Register
.

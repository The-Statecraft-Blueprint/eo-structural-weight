# item-207

By the authority vested in me as President by the Constitution and the laws of the United States of America, and in order to increase the number of rivers that the President may designate as American Heritage Rivers, it is hereby ordered that the second sentence of both section 2(d)(1) of Executive Order [EO-A] and of section 2(a) of Executive Order [EO-B] are amended by deleting "ten" and inserting "up to 20" in lieu thereof.
